Delete account

Unterwegs does not create accounts. Accounts exist only on the AdventureLog server you connect to. The app developer provides only the client; they own neither that server nor the data you put in it, and they run no public AdventureLog server. The operator of your server is responsible for your account and travel data. There are three ways.

1. In the app

  1. Open Settings → Account → Delete account. You must be online.
  2. Enter your username to confirm and, if your account has a password, your password. If your account has no password (for example it signs in only through SSO), the username is enough.
  3. Confirm the prompt (“Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted from the server”).

The app then asks your server to delete the account. What exactly is deleted (collections, locations, photos, keys, …) is decided by your AdventureLog server; the app deletes nothing else on the server. Afterwards the app signs out and always deletes all local data (see below). Administrators (staff accounts) cannot delete themselves.

2. On your AdventureLog server

Sign in on your server's website and delete the account in the settings there. The same rules apply: your username to confirm, a password only for accounts that have one. The app signs out at its next request, because the key is no longer valid.

3. Ask the operator

If someone else runs your server, or you can't use ways 1 and 2, ask that person to delete your account and data. For travel data, the server operator is responsible, not the app developer. If you have an account on the developer's private server for friends and family, the developer is your operator: write to __CONTACT_EMAIL__.

What is deleted on the device

  • When you delete the account in the app: always all local data: the API key, the cached database, downloaded files (PDFs, photos), image cache, offline maps and exports.
  • When you sign out: the API key and the cached database. The app asks whether to keep or delete downloads, photos and maps; optionally the key is also deleted on the server.
  • When you uninstall the app: everything.

Data with the developer

Through the app, the developer stores no travel data and no account. Builds with an update server send a pseudonymous installation ID, device model, operating system and crash flags (privacy policy, section 3). To have these deleted, write to __CONTACT_EMAIL__. The app does not show the installation ID; instead name your device, operating system and roughly when you used the app. Uninstalling the app removes the ID from the device; a later install gets a new one. The old record on the update server stays until it is deleted.
