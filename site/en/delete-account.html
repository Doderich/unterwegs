Delete account

Unterwegs has no account with the developer. Your account belongs to your AdventureLog server. There are three ways.

1. In the app

  1. Open Settings → Account → Delete account. You must be online.
  2. Enter your username to confirm and, if your account has a password, your password.
  3. Confirm the prompt (“Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted from the server”).

The app then asks your server to delete the account. What exactly is deleted (collections, locations, photos, keys, …) is decided by your AdventureLog server; the app deletes nothing else on the server. Afterwards the app signs out and always deletes all local data (see below). Administrators (staff accounts) cannot delete themselves.

2. On your AdventureLog server

Sign in on your server's website and delete the account in the account settings there. The app signs out at its next request, because the key is no longer valid.

3. Ask the operator

If someone else runs your server (or your account exists only through SSO), ask that person to delete your account and data. For travel data, the server operator is responsible, not the app developer.

What is deleted on the device

  • When you delete the account in the app: always all local data: the API key, the cached database, downloaded files (PDFs, photos), image cache, offline maps and exports.
  • When you sign out: the API key and the cached database. The app asks whether to keep or delete downloads, photos and maps; optionally the key is also deleted on the server.
  • When you uninstall the app: everything.

Data with the developer

The developer stores no travel data and no account. Builds with an update server send a pseudonymous installation ID, device model, operating system and crash flags (privacy policy, section 3). To have these deleted, write to privacy@example.org with the installation ID, if you know it.
