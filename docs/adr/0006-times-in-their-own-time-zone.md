# Every time is shown in its own time zone

The server stores times in UTC and the collection has no time zone. The app shows each time in the zone of the thing it belongs to (a lodging's zone, a transportation's departure or arrival zone, a visit's zone), never the phone's, so a flight reads "13:00 Frankfurt → 10:45 Tokyo" wherever the phone is. "Today" follows the zone of tonight's lodging, falling back to the collection time zone (the zone most of its lodgings are in). Times typed into the app are read in the same zone and converted to UTC (`src/time/zonedInput.ts`): the pickers work in the phone's wall-clock components, which the app re-reads in the object's zone, because Android's picker ignores a time-zone setting. All-day visits are stored as midnight UTC of their date and keep that date.

Location sun times and visited-today use the location's visit zone, nearby lodging/transportation zone or country-based fallback before falling back to the collection. Lodging/transportation search selections now suggest a zone from coordinates using a compact offline lookup, restricted to zones the server accepts. It is an editable approximation near boundaries, chosen so suggestions work without a separate time-zone service.
